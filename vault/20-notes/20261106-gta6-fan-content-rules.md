# GTA 6 fan content: what Rockstar's rules actually allow

> Rockstar tolerates **non-commercial** fan use of its material, bars using its content as
> input to **generative AI tools**, and has (per community reports) issued copyright strikes
> on AI-generated GTA 6 imagery. The safe lane for AI-made GTA 6 shorts is original,
> inspired-by visuals plus commentary, not re-created Rockstar assets.

## What we know (primary sources, read 2026-11-06)

**Rockstar Support, "Policy on posting copyrighted Rockstar Games material" (updated 2025-01-10)**
- "Take-Two Interactive does not object to our fans using materials for non-commercial uses
  in a manner which does not intentionally spoil the plot for others."
- "Non-commercial" means you do not make money from the footage or use it to promote a
  product or service. Take-Two reserves the right to take material down at any time.
- Take-down triggers include: pre-release or leaked footage of any kind, spoilers and
  isolated cutscenes, in-game TV/comedy content shown in isolation, cheating/mod-menu
  promotion, unauthorized mods or ports, and "combining unrelated intellectual property".
- The policy "does not apply to the exploitation of Rockstar IP for film, TV, or streaming
  distribution; in music videos; advertisements; or ... digital publishing." Licensing goes
  through copyright@take2games.com.

**Rockstar Terms of Service (rockstargames.com/legal, §2.3 Restrictions)**
- The license does not allow you to "modify, create derivative works, commercialize, or
  otherwise exploit the Services (including the Content)".
- "Nothing in the limited license ... authorizes the use of the Services (including the
  Content) ... in any manner to develop, train, enhance, or provide source material for, or
  promote, any Generative AI Tools; and any such uses are hereby explicitly prohibited."
- "Generative AI Tools" is defined broadly: any AI/ML tool that generates "still or moving
  images, musical works, human voice emulation, audio material" from prompts or inputs.

**Everygen ToS / FAQ**
- "Only upload media you have the rights to use." Monetization is allowed but each
  platform's rules on reused and AI-generated content still apply.

## What is claimed (not verified)
- Community posts say Rockstar is "officially giving out copyright strikes to AI generated
  photos and videos of GTA6". Plausible given the ToS language; no Rockstar statement found.
- A 2026 piece reported YouTube policy changes could demonetize GTA 6 gameplay showing
  violence against NPCs. Treat as unconfirmed until YouTube's own policy text is checked.

## Practical rules for this stack
1. **Never feed Rockstar material into Everygen**: no trailer frames, screenshots, artwork,
   logos, the GTA/Rockstar wordmarks, character likenesses (Jason, Lucia), or soundtrack.
   That is the one thing the ToS prohibits in plain words.
2. **Generate original, inspired-by worlds**: neon Florida coast, 80s/2020s Miami-style
   skylines, pastel art-deco, palm-lined highways. Describe the vibe, never the trademark.
3. **Commentary and news formats are the safest commercial lane**: a faceless "news desk"
   or explainer short where the visuals are original and any official footage is shown
   briefly, unaltered, and clearly as commentary. Even then, monetized use of official
   footage sits outside the fan policy; keep official clips minimal or absent.
4. **No pre-release or leaked footage, ever.** Launch is 2026-11-19; pre-load 2026-11-10.
5. **Label AI content** where the platform asks (YouTube altered/synthetic media disclosure,
   TikTok AI label).
6. **Keep titles honest**: "GTA 6 inspired", "what to expect", "explained", not
   "leaked gameplay" or anything implying official footage.

Confidence: **high** on the policy text (quoted from primary sources), **low** on how
aggressively it is enforced week to week.
